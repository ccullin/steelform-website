# Steelform Engineering Website Strategy

## Purpose

The website exists to:

1. Generate qualified enquiries.
2. Increase organic search traffic.
3. Demonstrate capability and experience.
4. Establish authority in heavy fabrication and excavator attachments.
5. Create a scalable content asset that compounds in value over time.

The website is not a digital brochure.

It is a search engine asset designed around customer intent.

## How Customers Search

Customers search for solutions to problems.

Examples:

- excavator bucket manufacturer
- mining bucket repair
- bucket rebuild specialist
- hardox bucket supplier
- dragline bucket repair
- excavator attachment manufacturer
- heavy fabrication contractor
- wear package replacement

Customers rarely search for:

- industries served
- company history
- about us

---

## Content Strategy

The website is built around five content types.

### Solutions

Primary SEO assets targeting purchase intent.

Examples:

- Excavator Buckets
- Excavator Attachments
- Bucket Repairs & Rebuilds
- Heavy Fabrication
- Mining Equipment
- Contract Manufacturing

### Projects

Proof of capability.

Projects demonstrate:

- Experience
- Quality
- Australian made
- Industry knowledge
- Equipment knowledge

### Articles

Educational content targeting informational searches.

Examples:

- Bucket Rebuild vs Replacement
- Hardox vs Bisalloy
- Selecting the Correct Excavator Bucket
- Bucket Wear Reduction Strategies

### Industries

Contextual content linking solutions and projects.

Examples:

- Mining
- Quarrying
- Earthmoving
- Construction
- Recycling

### Equipment Types

Long-tail SEO opportunities.

Examples:

- CAT 336
- Komatsu PC450
- Hitachi ZX350

## SEO Strategy

Primary focus:

- Solution pages
- Project pages
- Knowledge articles

Supporting focus:

- Industry pages
- Equipment pages

SEO success is driven by:

1. High-quality solution pages.
2. Regular project publishing.
3. Consistent knowledge articles.
4. Strong internal linking.
5. Structured relationships between content.
